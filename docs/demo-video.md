# Demo video: script and shot list

Written 13 Oct 2026.

**The portal's rule:** show the working product, not slides or a code walkthrough. Up to 3 minutes, hosted on YouTube, Loom, Vimeo or similar.

So every second is the live site or a real transaction on the explorer. No code, no terminal, no diagrams. Target length **2:50**. A **2:15 cut** is marked for when a scene cannot be recorded.

## Before you record

- **Use a fresh pet made for the recording**, with made-up texts. Never record real private messages.
- **Make it on the browser you will record in, and do the whole recording in one sitting.** Since tonight's passkey fix, a browser remembers the passkey it just made and asks for it by name.
- **Warm the town up.** Keep `/watch` open for 5 to 10 minutes first so the chronicle is full and some pairs are close.
- **Close every other tab showing the town** when you are done. Each open tab spends the free model allowance.
- Record at 1920×1080, browser zoom 100%, bookmarks bar hidden, notifications off.
- Tabs to have ready:
  1. `https://stand-in-ashen.vercel.app` (landing, in a window that is signed out)
  2. `https://stand-in-ashen.vercel.app/world` (signed in as the recording pet)
  3. A second device or browser profile with a second pet, for scenes 6 and 7

## What each scene needs

| Scene | Needs | If it cannot be recorded |
|---|---|---|
| 1, 2 Landing and `/watch` | Nothing | Always recordable |
| 3 Make a pet | One passkey prompt | Required. Without it there is no video |
| 4 Dials | One prompt | Required |
| 5 Weather | One prompt, clear sky | Required: it is the best 20 seconds |
| 6 Introduction | Two real pets at Kindred, two devices | Use the fallback below |
| 7 Meeting in person | Two pets, a phone camera | Drop it (*cut for 2:15*) |
| 8 Letter | Leave the town for 2 minutes while another tab keeps it running | Drop it (*cut for 2:15*) |
| 9 Soul vault | Two prompts to lock, one to unlock | Drop it, and do not mention the vault |

### Scene 6 fallback, if no two real pets reached Kindred

Show the consent card between your pet and a made-up resident, and say it: "With a made-up resident, my yes is enough and nothing goes on chain." Then, for the on-chain part, show a real record separately: on your pet's page open a friendship made in scene 7 and click **See the transaction**. Do not present one as the other.

To give two real pets a chance before recording: set both pace dials to the top, give them two shared interests, and keep the town open.

## Editing notes

- Speed up typing and waiting (2× to 4×). Never speed up the town reacting to the storm; that is the moment to let breathe.
- Cut every passkey prompt to about one second.
- If a transaction takes more than three seconds, cut the wait.
- Keep the total under 3:00 including any title card. A title card is not needed.

## What not to claim

- That two real people's pets were introduced, unless you recorded it.
- That the vault works across devices, unless you show it.
- That anything runs on mainnet. It is Monad testnet.
- That the town runs by itself. It moves only while someone is watching.
